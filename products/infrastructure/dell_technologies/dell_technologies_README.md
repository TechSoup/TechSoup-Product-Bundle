## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, Dell Technologies for Nonprofits provides access to discounted desktops, laptops, tablets, servers, and accessories. The program adds remote computer setup assistance for purchased systems, an assigned Dell Technologies adviser, and flexible payment and financing options via Dell Payment Solutions.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- Open to US 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries, validated through TechSoup.
- Any budget size; the offer is requested once and then provides ongoing access.
- Unlimited discounted purchases; coupons are applied automatically with the membership passcode.
- U.S. territories are ineligible.
- Flexible payment plans are available, including ownership, subscription/lifecycle, and pay-per-use models.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** None to enroll; physical space, power, and networking for the hardware purchased. Remote setup assistance is available for purchased systems.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to Moderate. Procurement and using the membership passcode is simple; deploying and configuring servers and fleets of devices takes IT capacity, though an assigned adviser and remote setup help.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. As a hardware program, ongoing work is the normal lifecycle of physical devices: deployment, warranty/repair handling, and eventual refresh or replacement.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** General. This is a hardware procurement program; security depends on how the purchased devices and servers are configured and maintained by the organization.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Solid for ongoing procurement, since access is unlimited and includes financing; however, hardware purchases are real recurring costs and devices require periodic refresh.
