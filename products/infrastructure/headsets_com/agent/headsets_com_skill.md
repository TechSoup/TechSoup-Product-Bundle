## Headsets.com Expert Knowledge
- There are per-fiscal-year quantity caps: up to 4 of each Leitner headset and 7 Overture headsets, so plan a full team's purchase against those limits before submitting.
- Match the connection type to the hardware (RJ9 for office phones vs. USB for PCs/Macs) before ordering, and remember the offer is U.S. organizations only, priced as a per-unit admin fee ($59-$245) paid to TechSoup.
