## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, Headsets.com provides wired and wireless office phone and computer headsets (the Leitner and Overture lines) at discounted administrative-fee pricing. The headsets work with office phones, PCs, and Macs, feature noise-canceling microphones, and are compatible with Skype and VoIP services.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- Open to US 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries, validated through TechSoup; U.S. organizations only.
- Any budget size.
- Quantity limits per fiscal year: up to 4 of each Leitner headset and 7 Overture headsets.
- Pricing is an administrative fee per unit paid to TechSoup ($59-$245 depending on the headset).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Compatible office phones, PCs, or Macs; headsets connect via RJ9 or USB. Wireless models offer up to a 350-foot range and 8-hour battery.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Plug-in headsets need little setup beyond matching the right connection (RJ9 or USB) to the device.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. As physical devices, the main considerations are charging wireless units and replacing hardware over time; models carry multi-year replacement warranties (2 to 5 years).

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** General. This is an audio hardware offer with no specific security functionality.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Good for the cost. Multi-year replacement warranties (2 to 5 years) extend the useful life of each unit, though headsets are consumable hardware that will eventually need replacing.
