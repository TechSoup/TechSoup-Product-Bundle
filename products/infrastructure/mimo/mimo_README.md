## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Mimo offers commercial-grade touchscreen displays, digital signage tablets, and casting devices to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries to build digital signage, self-service kiosks, and interactive applications. Available through TechSoup in both donated and discounted SKUs, the catalog includes commercial-grade LCD touchscreen monitors (7-inch and 10.1-inch), Android digital signage tablets, and the FlashCast emergency alert overlay device. Hardware supports VESA mounting and PoE and ships with one- to three-year warranties.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- **Eligibility:** Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Region:** United States.
- **Budget cap:** Any budget size (no operating-budget ceiling specified).
- **Quantity limits:** Unlimited discounted products.
- **Exclusions / restrictions:** Products may not be resold.
- **Payment model:** Mixed donation and discount. TechSoup admin fees range from $43 to $435 depending on the SKU.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Physical displays/tablets requiring procurement, mounting (VESA), power (including PoE setup where used), and network connectivity. Plan the signage/kiosk content and the app or service that will drive the screens.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. Mounting, cabling/PoE, and configuring digital signage or kiosk software requires hands-on IT effort beyond plugging in a single device.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. As deployed physical hardware (often in public-facing kiosks), units need ongoing power/network monitoring, warranty tracking (one to three years), content updates, and eventual replacement.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** General. The FlashCast device supports emergency alert overlays, but overall security depends on how kiosks and signage tablets are locked down and networked by the organization.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Unlimited discounted purchasing supports building out and maintaining a signage/kiosk fleet over time, though public-facing touchscreens face wear and will need eventual replacement.
