## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

USB Memory Direct Imprinted USB Flash Drives offers nonprofits discounted custom-imprinted USB 2.0/3.0 flash drives in volumes up to 500 or 2,500, including imprinting services and a lifetime warranty. Offered to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries through TechSoup, the program provides custom-imprinted drives in various styles and capacities (64 MB to 128 GB), imprinting from vector or raster artwork, and free email, phone, and chat support. It suits organizations producing branded drives for events, outreach, or fundraising.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- **Eligibility:** Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Region:** United States.
- **Budget cap:** Any budget size (no operating-budget ceiling).
- **Quantity limits:** Unlimited drives; offered in volumes up to 500 or up to 2,500 per order.
- **Exclusions / restrictions:** Organizations may not publicly advertise the exact discount amount.
- **Payment model:** Discount offer. TechSoup admin fee is $15 (volumes up to 500) or $35 (volumes up to 2,500), with a 20%+ discount paid directly to USB Memory Direct.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Provide imprint artwork (vector or raster) and place the order; drives arrive as finished physical media. Pre-loading or distribution of the drives is handled by the organization.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Ordering imprinted drives requires only artwork and a quantity selection; no IT configuration is needed.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. The drives carry a lifetime warranty and free support; there is no software or service to maintain, though the physical media itself must be stored and distributed.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** General. These are standard USB flash drives; any data security (encryption, contents) is the organization's responsibility, not a feature of the offer.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** The lifetime warranty supports durable use, but as removable storage media the drives are consumables subject to loss; this is a procurement offer rather than an ongoing service.
