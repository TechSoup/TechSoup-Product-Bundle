## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
New Relic's Observability for Good program provides nonprofits with deep insights into their software performance. It offers 1TB of free data ingest per month, up to 5 free full-platform users, and discounts on additional usage, helping IT teams monitor uptime, troubleshoot errors, and ensure system reliability.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to recognized 501(c)(3) charities globally.
- Requires application and verification through TechSoup.
- Cannot be used by K-12 schools, universities, or hospitals (unless they meet specific exceptions).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Hosted applications or infrastructure that can accept telemetry agents (e.g., APM agents, infrastructure monitoring).
- **IT Expertise Required:** High. Installing telemetry agents, configuring dashboards, and writing PromQL/NRQL queries requires an experienced DevOps or Site Reliability Engineer (SRE).
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. The platform largely runs itself once configured, but dashboard maintenance and alert tuning are ongoing tasks to prevent alert fatigue.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** SOC2 Type 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP Authorized. Configurable data obfuscation to prevent ingestion of PII.
- **AI Functionality:** New Relic AI (AIOps) provides automated anomaly detection and incident correlation. Generative AI assistant (New Relic Grok) is available to query telemetry using natural language.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Medium/High. 1TB of data is highly sustainable for most small-to-medium nonprofits, but a sudden spike in traffic (e.g., during a major fundraising event) could inadvertently trigger high overage costs if not capped.
