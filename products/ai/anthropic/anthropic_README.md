## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Anthropic provides eligible nonprofits with discounted access to Claude and potential credit grants to support the implementation of generative AI for mission-driven work.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- Verified 501(c)(3) status required.
- Focus on organizations addressing social and humanitarian impact.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** API key management or platform-level access.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low to Moderate. Requires ongoing monitoring of credit usage and safety guardrails.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** Constitutional AI approach focusing on safety and reduced hallucinations.
- **AI Functionality:** Advanced reasoning, long context window support, and high safety standards.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High, provides cost-effective access to cutting-edge models.
