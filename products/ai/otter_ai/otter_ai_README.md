## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Otter.ai for Nonprofits provides discounted access to Otter for Teams, an AI-powered automated recording and transcription service for meetings, interviews, and lectures, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup. It generates automated real-time transcription from live or recorded audio and video, identifies speakers in a way that improves over time, and supports keyword search and summaries so teams can quickly find what was said. Transcripts can be edited, tagged, and exported to formats such as TXT, PDF, SRT, and MP3 for sharing.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- No budget cap: organizations of any budget size are eligible.
- Quantity limits: one initial subscription over the organization's lifetime, plus one renewal per fiscal year.
- Requires commitment to a 1-year service contract.
- A credit card is required to complete the order.
- Payment model: a discount (72% off the initial Otter for Teams subscription, 50% off renewal) paid directly to Otter.ai, with a TechSoup administrative fee of $27-$39.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** A web browser and access to the meeting/recording audio or video sources to be transcribed; a credit card for the subscription.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Otter is a hosted SaaS transcription service with no infrastructure to deploy or manage.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. The vendor maintains the platform; ongoing effort is limited to managing the subscription and renewing per fiscal year under the 1-year contract.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Cloud-hosted service; capabilities cover transcript editing, tagging, and online sharing, so organizations should manage who has access to shared transcripts. No specific certifications are claimed in this offer.
- **AI Functionality:** Core to this offer. Otter uses AI for automated real-time transcription, speaker identification that learns over time, keyword search, and summaries.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** With one lifetime initial subscription and a discounted renewal each fiscal year, the offer suits organizations prepared to maintain an ongoing annual contract for transcription needs.
