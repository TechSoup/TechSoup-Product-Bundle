## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
DonorPerfect is a comprehensive fundraising CRM designed to help nonprofits manage donor relationships, process online gifts, and track grants. They offer specialized pricing tiers scaled to the number of donor records a nonprofit maintains.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Broadly available to 501(c)(3) charities.
- Pricing is heavily tiered based on record count (e.g., under 1,000 records vs. 10,000+ records).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Clean CSV exports of past donor history for data migration.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to Moderate. UI is slightly dated but highly functional without coding knowledge.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Requires deduplication and standard CRM hygiene.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** PCI Level 1 compliant for payment processing. Standard role-based access control.
- **AI Functionality:** Basic predictive algorithms for ask amounts, but limited generative AI.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Established player in the market with very stable pricing models that won't suddenly spike unless donor records explode.
