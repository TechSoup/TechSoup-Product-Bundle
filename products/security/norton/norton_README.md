## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Norton for Nonprofits provides donated consumer-grade security — Norton 360, Secure VPN, and Small Business plans — well suited to unmanaged or "bring your own device" environments. Organizations pay a small administrative fee per subscription through TechSoup.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries in the US.
- **Budget Cap:** Under $10M annual budget.
- **Staff Limit:** 50 or fewer employees.
- **Excluded:** Academic, advocacy, and religious-evangelizing organizations, and certain membership/social clubs.

### Norton Donation Program for Domestic Violence Survivors
A separate, fully donated Norton track (no administrative fee) lets domestic-violence advocacy organizations distribute Norton 360 Deluxe and Secure VPN subscriptions **directly to survivors** for their personal online safety. It requires Safe Shelter Collaborative membership and direct service to survivors; subscriptions go to the survivors served, not to organization staff (up to 100 per product per fiscal year, max 50 per request).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Individual per-device installation; no central management console.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Device protection plus Secure VPN, Dark Web Monitoring, firewall, password manager, and 50–500 GB PC cloud backup in Deluxe tiers.
- **AI Functionality:** Predictive AI for malware detection.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High for very small teams, but lacks centralized management for larger networks.
