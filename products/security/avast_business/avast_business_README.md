## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Avast Business offers its cloud-based CloudCare security suite — managed antivirus, content filtering, and patch management — to nonprofits and libraries as donated annual subscriptions through TechSoup, for a small per-product administrative fee.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Budget Cap:** None — organizations of any annual budget size are eligible.
- **Restrictions:** Not available to individuals, and not available if your organization has engaged with Avast sales or a reseller in the past six months.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Create a CloudCare account and deploy agents to the endpoints you want to protect.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to moderate — administration is handled from a single web dashboard.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low; antivirus definitions and patches are managed centrally and applied automatically.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** Cloud-managed antivirus with content filtering across 60M+ presorted sites, patch management for the OS and applications, and historical activity/threat reporting.
- **AI Functionality:** Cloud-based threat intelligence informs malware detection across the managed fleet.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High for distributed or remote teams that need centrally managed protection without on-premises infrastructure; renews annually through TechSoup.
