## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
1Password offers a 50% discount on its Teams and Business plans for registered nonprofits. It provides a centralized, secure platform for teams to manage and share passwords, secure notes, and MFA tokens without exposing raw credentials.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to recognized 501(c)(3) charities.
- Requires submission of tax documentation via their non-profit portal.
- Discount applies perpetually as long as status is maintained.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond an admin email address.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. The interface is highly intuitive for rollout.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Admins only need to offboard users upon departure to maintain security.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** End-to-end encryption (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256), SOC2 Type 2, ISO 27001. Secret Key architecture prevents server-side breaches.
- **AI Functionality:** None that processes vault contents.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Easily exportable vaults if a migration is needed, and low ongoing maintenance overhead.
