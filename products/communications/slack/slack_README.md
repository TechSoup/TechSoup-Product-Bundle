## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Slack provides powerful real-time messaging and collaboration. Nonprofits with 250 or fewer members can upgrade to the Pro plan for free. Larger organizations or those seeking the Business+ plan receive an 85% discount.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Must be a registered 501(c)(3) or equivalent.
- Requires validation via TechSoup or Goodstack.
- Excludes legislative/political organizations and most schools (use Slack for Education).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None for basic chat; SSO requires Business+ tier.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** SOC2 Type 2, ISO 27001. Data residency options available on Enterprise Grid.
- **AI Functionality:** Slack AI (generative search, summaries) available as a paid add-on.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Deep integrations with other VKB tools (Asana, Google) prevent siloed data.
