## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Ultralingua is translation dictionary and thesaurus reference software for Mac and Windows, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup for multilingual communication and teaching. It covers English, French, German, Italian, Latin, Portuguese, and Spanish, with bilingual dictionaries offering up to 295,000 translations. It also includes verb conjugation tools across multiple tenses and hotkey lookups from within documents.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries in the US; any budget size.
- Up to 40 licenses per fiscal year.
- Most advocacy groups and foundations are ineligible.
- Donated software; a $7 admin fee applies per license.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Mac or Windows desktop computers; the software runs locally with hotkey lookups from within documents.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
