## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
QuestionPro for Nonprofits provides a donated 1-year subscription to QuestionPro online survey software, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, with up to two accounts for collecting and analyzing survey data from constituents, stakeholders, and employees. It offers 30+ question types and flexible branching logic, along with email list management and social publishing. Results come back as real-time reports, infographics, and response maps.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries in the US; any budget size.
- One subscription per fiscal year.
- Existing or former QuestionPro subscribers are ineligible.
- Donated software; a TechSoup admin fee of $48-$97 applies per 1-year subscription.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None for building and distributing surveys; offline mobile data collection is available on iOS and Android.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Cloud-hosted with an annual subscription to renew.
