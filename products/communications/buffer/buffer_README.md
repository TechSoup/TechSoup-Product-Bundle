## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Buffer gives nonprofits a 50% discount on any of its social media management plans through TechSoup, letting teams plan, schedule, and publish content across major social channels from one dashboard. It includes multi-channel publishing, analytics and reporting, and an AI assistant for content creation, plus a link-in-bio landing page tool and iOS/Android apps.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Any budget size is eligible.
- One offer per organization lifetime; existing subscribers may request a discounted renewal.
- A credit card is required, and the 50% discount applies to the plan paid to Buffer.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** None; web-based with mobile apps.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low to Moderate, driven by ongoing content scheduling rather than administration.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** Connects to social accounts through standard channel authentication; team collaboration and post approval workflows support internal controls.
- **AI Functionality:** Buffer AI Assistant for content creation and post ideas.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Simplifies multi-channel social management with a lasting recurring discount.
