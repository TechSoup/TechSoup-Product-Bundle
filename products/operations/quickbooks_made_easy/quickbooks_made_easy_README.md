## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

QuickBooks Made Easy (founded by CPA Gregg S. Bossen) provides nonprofit-specific training and support for Intuit's QuickBooks products, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup at discounted rates. It is a third-party trainer, not Intuit, and does not include QuickBooks software itself. The offer covers video-based training on nonprofit QuickBooks tasks, live webinar series (online and desktop versions), and downloadable follow-along handbooks, with optional U.S.-based tech support plans.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Video training products are limited to organizations with budgets up to $2M.
- Tickets to live events / the webinar series are available to organizations of any budget size.
- Quantity limits: one video training product per fiscal year; unlimited tickets.
- This is a third-party trainer, not Intuit, and the offer does NOT include QuickBooks software.
- Payment model is a discount via TechSoup admin fee of $109-$349.
- US organizations only.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** A separately licensed copy of QuickBooks (online or desktop) to apply the training to; this offer is training only. A web browser is needed for video and webinar access.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. These are training materials and live sessions, not software to deploy.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. No ongoing technical upkeep; content is consumed as training.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** Not applicable; this is training content, not a data-handling platform.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Training builds durable in-house bookkeeping capability, but remember it is decoupled from the QuickBooks software your organization must license and maintain separately.
