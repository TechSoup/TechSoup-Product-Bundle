## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Springly is cloud-based all-in-one membership management software for nonprofits, offered through TechSoup at a 30% discount. It centers on a CRM with unlimited customizable contact fields, membership drives with online fee collection, and secure online payments. A full accounting suite (or QuickBooks integration), a website builder, email newsletters, and event management round out the platform.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Any budget size is eligible.
- Limit of one discount within the lifetime of the organization.
- Payment model is a 30% discount paid to Springly, with a $15 TechSoup admin fee.
- US organizations only.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond a web browser; it is a hosted, cloud-based platform.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. No installation; configuring membership tiers, the website builder, and optional QuickBooks integration is light operations work.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Vendor-managed SaaS; your team maintains member data and content.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Offers secure online payments; broader controls are not detailed in this offer.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Consolidating CRM, membership, accounting, payments, website, and events in one tool reduces silos, but note the discount is one-per-organization-lifetime, so the post-discount price applies thereafter.
