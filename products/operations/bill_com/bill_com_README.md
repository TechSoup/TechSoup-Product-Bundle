## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Through TechSoup, BILL offers nonprofits a 40% discount on its cloud-based payment and invoicing platform for managing accounts payable and receivable. Finance teams can send domestic and international ACH and credit card payments, set up automated recurring invoicing, and route bills through AP approval workflows with custom roles. AI-powered invoice data entry reduces manual keying of bill details.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Budget Cap:** Any budget size is eligible.
- **Quantity Limit:** One per fiscal year.
- **New vs. Existing:** Organizations with a paid BILL subscription in the last 6 months are ineligible.
- **Payment Model:** Discounted subscription (40% off); a credit card or bank account is required.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** A linked bank account or credit card; an existing QuickBooks or Xero ledger is recommended to take advantage of the two-way sync.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low for setup. Configuring approval workflows and custom roles is primarily a finance-process task rather than an IT one.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Approval roles, recurring invoices, and the accounting sync need periodic review to stay accurate.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** SOC 2 and HIPAA compliant. Custom approval roles provide internal control and separation of duties over payments.
- **AI Functionality:** AI-powered invoice data entry extracts bill details to reduce manual entry.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Two-way sync with QuickBooks and Xero keeps it integrated with the standard nonprofit accounting stack, limiting data silos.
