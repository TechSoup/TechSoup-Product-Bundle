## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, AvePoint provides Office 365 and SharePoint migration and management software to eligible U.S. organizations, available as an initial subscription with a separate renewal product. It enables centralized SharePoint Online management, site restructuring and migration, and policy enforcement with alerting. Global reporting on settings and security gives administrators visibility across their environment.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries (U.S.).
- **Budget Cap:** Annual operating budget of $20M or less.
- **Quantity Limit:** An initial subscription plus a renewal product; the admin fee covers a subscription for 10 users.
- **New vs. Existing:** Current or former AvePoint subscribers within the last 12 months are ineligible.
- **Exclusions:** Legislative/political and advocacy organizations are not eligible.
- **Payment Model:** Donated software for a $39.00 admin fee per subscription (10 users).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** An existing Microsoft 365 / SharePoint Online tenant to manage and migrate.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate to High. SharePoint site restructuring, migration, and policy enforcement require an administrator comfortable with Microsoft 365 governance.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Policies, alerts, and reporting need ongoing administration to stay aligned with the tenant.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Provides policy enforcement with alerting and global reporting on settings and security across SharePoint Online.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Moderate. Valuable for organizations already standardized on SharePoint Online, with a defined renewal path, but its usefulness is tied entirely to a continuing Microsoft 365 investment.
