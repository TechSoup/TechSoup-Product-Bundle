## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Unity is a real-time 3D content creation platform offered to nonprofits through TechSoup as a one-year subscription activating up to 50 seats to build games, VR/AR experiences, and training simulations for educational and mission purposes. It supports real-time 3D/2D development across platforms and visual graph-based scripting, plus cloud diagnostics with automatic error capture and user feedback.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries; any budget size.
- Unlimited products.
- Hospitals are ineligible.
- US territories are ineligible.
- Non-commercial use only.
- Donated 1-year subscription for 50 users; a $180 admin fee applies.

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Workstations capable of real-time 3D development; cloud diagnostics integrate with Slack, Discord, and Jira.
- **IT Expertise Required:** High. Real-time 3D/2D development and VR/AR work require specialized skills, though visual graph-based scripting lowers the coding barrier.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. The subscription activates up to 50 seats for one year and must be renewed annually.
