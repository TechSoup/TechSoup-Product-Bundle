## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
VideoScribe is whiteboard animation software offered to nonprofits through TechSoup for creating explainer, promotional, and instructional videos that appear hand-drawn on a whiteboard. It provides whiteboard animation with customizable hands, pens, and erasers, a simplified interface with prebuilt templates, and a library of 6,000+ royalty-free images and audio. No prior video editing experience is required.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries in the US; any budget size.
- Up to 5 single-user subscriptions per fiscal year.
- Existing paid subscribers are ineligible.
- Payment model: $0 admin fee to TechSoup; the 50% discount is paid directly to Sparkol ($17.50/mo or $84/yr).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None; the simplified interface and templates require no prior video editing experience.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Single-user subscriptions with export to MOV, WMV, MP4, and AVI and direct sharing to YouTube/Vimeo.
