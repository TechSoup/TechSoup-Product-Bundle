## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

LinkedIn for Nonprofits provides a suite of tools and resources to help organizations find talent, engage donors, and build awareness. This includes free volunteer job postings, ad grants for specific impact areas, and discounted premium products like Sales Navigator and LinkedIn Learning.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- **Status:** Open to verified 501(c)(3) organizations or international equivalents.
- **Verification:** Verification is typically managed through the LinkedIn platform and may involve third-party partners like Goodstack.
- **Specific Programs:** Ad grants are often restricted to organizations focused on Economic Opportunity, DEI, or Environmental Sustainability.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Low. Standard LinkedIn profile and company page.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Requires active page management and engagement to be effective.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** High. Standard LinkedIn enterprise security.
- **AI Functionality:** LinkedIn provides AI-powered recruiting insights and AI-assisted profile/post drafting tools.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. LinkedIn is the primary professional network for nonprofit talent and networking.
