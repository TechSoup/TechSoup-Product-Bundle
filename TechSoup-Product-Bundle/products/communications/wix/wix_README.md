## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Wix is a website management platform offered to nonprofits through TechSoup via discounted coupon codes for building and hosting mobile-friendly websites with no technical knowledge required. It provides drag-and-drop building with the Wix Editor and Wix ADI, web hosting and storage with a free one-year custom domain voucher, and secure e-commerce to sell products, accept donations, and run events and memberships. The offer covers Premium Core, Premium Business, Premium Business Elite, and Wix Studio plans.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- All organization types may apply, but only 501(c)(3) nonprofits and IMLS-listed public libraries are eligible.
- Unlimited requests.
- Organizations must be willing to provide a case study or testimonial.
- Requires TechSoup validation; discounts are delivered as coupon codes applied to the Wix plan.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None; no coding knowledge needed.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Hosting, storage, and updates are managed by Wix.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Managed hosting with integrated donation campaigns via the Wix Donations App; e-commerce supports products, donations, events, and memberships.
- **AI Functionality:** Built-in AI design and content tools, including Wix ADI and AI capabilities within Wix Studio.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. SEO, analytics, and marketing tools plus no-code editing make it easy to hand off between staff and volunteers.
