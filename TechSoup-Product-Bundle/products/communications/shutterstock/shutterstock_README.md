## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Shutterstock for Nonprofits provides discount coupon codes for licensed stock media, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup for use in websites, ads, social media, and marketing materials. It gives access to millions of licensed photos, vectors, and illustrations, plus licensed 4K, HD, and SD stock video. Web-based image editing is available through Shutterstock Editor, and collections can be shared.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries; any budget size.
- Up to 12 FLEX coupon codes and 12 Video coupon codes per fiscal year.
- Monthly-billed individual plans only.
- A credit card is required.
- Payment model: a $5-$10 admin fee is paid to TechSoup, while the 35% FLEX subscription / 25% video pack discount is paid to Shutterstock.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None; licensed assets are downloaded and used directly, with optional editing in the web-based Shutterstock Editor.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Coupon-based, with monthly billing on individual plans.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Licensed stock content reduces copyright risk in nonprofit marketing materials.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** A flexible, coupon-driven way to license media as needed, capped at 12 FLEX and 12 Video codes per fiscal year.
