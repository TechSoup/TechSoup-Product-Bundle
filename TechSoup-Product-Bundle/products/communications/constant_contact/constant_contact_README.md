## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Constant Contact is an online marketing and outreach platform offered to nonprofits through TechSoup at a discount. It provides email campaign creation with templates and an AI Campaign Builder, constituent list management with CRM integration, and engagement tracking. Organizations can also run automation and segmentation, social media and SMS outreach, and online surveys, polls, and event invitations.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries; any budget size.
- **New Users Only:** Existing Constant Contact subscribers are ineligible.
- One offer per organization.
- Requires TechSoup validation; the discount applies to the Constant Contact subscription.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate, driven by list hygiene, segmentation, and ongoing campaign management.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Standard email marketing platform security; CRM integration keeps constituent data centralized.
- **AI Functionality:** AI Campaign Builder for drafting email campaigns.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. A reliable, broadly used platform for donor and constituent communications.
