## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
iFax is a HIPAA-compliant online faxing platform offered to nonprofits through TechSoup at a 50% discount on its Plus and Professional subscriptions. It lets organizations send and receive faxes securely from any web browser or an iOS, Android, Windows, or macOS app, with a dedicated phone number and notifications. A built-in document scanner and cloud import round out the workflow.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries; any budget size.
- Existing iFax subscribers are ineligible.
- One of each offer per fiscal year.
- Payment model: a $13-$23 admin fee is paid to TechSoup, and the 50% discount applies to the monthly or annual subscription paid directly to iFax.
- Plan capacity: Plus supports up to 500 pages/month; Professional supports 1,000+ pages/month.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond a web browser or the iOS/Android/Windows/macOS app; Professional adds enterprise options such as API, sub-accounts, and unlimited fax numbers.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low for basic faxing; Moderate if using Professional API and sub-account features.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Cloud-hosted with no on-premise fax hardware to maintain.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** HIPAA and GLBA compliant, suitable for organizations handling protected health or financial information.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** A stable, compliant replacement for physical fax infrastructure with predictable subscription costs.
