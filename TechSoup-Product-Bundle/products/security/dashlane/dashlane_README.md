## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Dashlane is a password manager and digital wallet that securely stores passwords, payment methods, and other private information for an organization's staff. Through TechSoup, eligible nonprofits get a 50% discount on Dashlane Team or Business plans.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries in the US.
- **Budget Cap:** None.
- **Quantity Limit:** One subscription per fiscal year (July 1 – June 30).
- **Restrictions:** Existing Dashlane subscribers and U.S. territories are not eligible. After paying TechSoup's administrative fee, you pay Dashlane directly for the discounted subscription.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Minimal — invite staff from the web-based admin console; users install the browser extension or apps.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low; onboarding and monitoring are handled from the admin console, with SSO available to reduce ongoing credential management.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Zero-knowledge encrypted vault with auto-fill, secure password sharing with individuals or groups, and separation of business and personal credentials; SAML-based single sign-on (SSO) on the Business plan.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to the offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Cross-platform (web, Windows, Mac, iOS, Android) with central administration, suited to organizations standardizing credential hygiene.
