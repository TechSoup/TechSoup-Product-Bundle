## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Ring Devices for Nonprofits provides donated smart home security hardware (battery video doorbells and outdoor cams, sold in bundles of six) to nonprofits supporting domestic violence survivors to enhance safety planning. Offered through TechSoup, the devices deliver motion-activated HD video doorbells and outdoor cameras, color night vision and live view, and two-way audio with noise cancellation. The battery-powered devices come with a Ring Lifetime Basic Plan.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- **Eligibility:** Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries, but the organization must directly serve domestic violence (DV) survivors.
- **Region:** United States.
- **Budget cap:** Any budget size (no operating-budget ceiling).
- **Quantity limits:** Up to 8 bundles per year, on a December 2-December 1 cycle (each bundle is 6 devices).
- **Approval requirement:** Must complete the Ring Exceptional Eligibility Request Form, approved by NNEDV.
- **Distribution requirement:** Devices must be distributed to survivors per the Ring Donation Acknowledgment.
- **Payment model:** Donation. TechSoup admin fee is $60.00 per bundle of 6.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Physical battery-powered devices that require home Wi-Fi, a smart device, and the Ring app to operate. Because devices are distributed to survivors, each end recipient must have a compatible Wi-Fi/smartphone setup.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. The hardware is consumer-grade, but the program involves an approval workflow (Ring Exceptional Eligibility Request Form via NNEDV), distribution logistics, and helping survivors install and connect devices.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Battery-powered devices require recharging, app/account management, and per-recipient support; the nonprofit must also track bundle limits and the donation acknowledgment process.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** Sensitive. Because devices support DV survivor safety planning and are distributed to vulnerable individuals, account, location, and footage privacy must be handled with extreme care under the program's NNEDV-approved process.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Devices include a Ring Lifetime Basic Plan, supporting ongoing use, but rely on each recipient's Wi-Fi and smart device and on the nonprofit's continued capacity to distribute and support them.
