## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, the AWS Nonprofit Credit Program provides promotional credits toward usage fees for AWS on-demand cloud services for eligible US nonprofits and libraries. Standard credits are tiered by budget ($1,000, $2,000, or $5,000), and the program spans compute via Amazon EC2 and container services, storage via Amazon EBS, EFS, and S3, and databases including RDS, Aurora, DynamoDB, and Redshift. A companion track offers $5,000 in AWS credits to select non-501(c)(3) organization types such as 501(c)(8), (c)(9), and (c)(12), cross-referencing the standard tiered nonprofit credit offers.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to US 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries validated through TechSoup; a separate track extends eligibility to select 501(c) designations beyond (c)(3), such as (c)(8), (c)(9), and (c)(12).
- One grant per fiscal year.
- Credit amount is tiered by annual budget: under $10M = $1,000; $10M-$50M = $2,000; over $50M = $5,000. The non-501(c)(3) track grants $5,000.
- Goodwill and United Way affiliates and educational institutions are ineligible.
- Credits are valid for 12 months toward on-demand services and are stackable with the AWS Free Tier for new customers.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** An AWS account in good standing to which credits can be applied.
- **IT Expertise Required:** High. Requires cloud architecture knowledge to provision and manage on-demand compute, storage, and database services responsibly.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** High. Active monitoring of spend is needed; Basic Support is free, and CloudWatch billing alerts help avoid overruns once the 12-month credits are exhausted.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** AWS operates a shared responsibility model: AWS secures the underlying infrastructure while the organization must configure secure access to its provisioned services.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer. The credits apply to general on-demand cloud services rather than a specific AI capability.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Honest caution: credits are time-limited (12 months) and tiered, so organizations that grow beyond the credited amount transition to standard commercial on-demand pricing.
