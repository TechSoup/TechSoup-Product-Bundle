## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Google Workspace for Nonprofits provides access to Google's suite of cloud productivity apps (Gmail, Docs, Drive, Meet) for free. The base non-profit tier allows unlimited users with 30GB of storage per user, and access to shared drives.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Organizations must be registered as a valid charity.
- Requires TechSoup verification.
- Excludes K-12 schools, universities, hospitals, and government entities (which have separate Google programs).
- NTEE Codes: Broad eligibility for 501(c)(3) organizations.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Domain ownership and DNS configuration.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to Moderate. Google Admin console is generally user-friendly for basic provisioning.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Automatic updates and simple user management reduce IT burden.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** SOC2, ISO 27001, HIPAA compliant (requires BAA signing). Strong MFA and device management included.
- **AI Functionality:** Gemini AI features available as add-ons. Core workspace data is not used for generalized AI training.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Widespread familiarity reduces training costs. Google Takeout allows for standard data export.
