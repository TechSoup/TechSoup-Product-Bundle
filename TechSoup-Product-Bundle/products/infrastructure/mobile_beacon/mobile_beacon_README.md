## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Mobile Beacon provides mobile hotspots, tablets, Chromebooks, and SIM cards bundled with low-cost national 5G/4G broadband service on the T-Mobile network, connecting nonprofits to the internet from any covered location. Offered to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries through TechSoup, it includes 4G and 5G mobile hotspots, 5G tablets and Chromebooks, and unlimited data with no caps or throttling. The bundled service runs $120/year per device and supports hotspot lending programs.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- **Eligibility:** Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Region:** United States; the organization must be within the coverage area.
- **Budget cap:** Any budget size (no operating-budget ceiling specified).
- **Quantity limits:** Per-fiscal-year limits apply (e.g., 11 hotspots, 11 tablets, 11 Chromebooks, 10 SIMs).
- **Payment model:** Mixed donation and discount. TechSoup admin fees range from $9 to $328 per device, plus a required prepaid $120/year Mobile Beacon broadband service per device.
- **Service requirement:** Each device requires the prepaid annual broadband service to function.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Physical devices (hotspots, tablets, Chromebooks, SIMs) requiring procurement, activation on the Mobile Beacon/T-Mobile service, and verification of T-Mobile coverage at the intended use locations. BYOD SIM cards require a compatible device.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low-to-Moderate. Hotspots and SIM activation are relatively straightforward, but managing a fleet (and a lending program) across many devices and renewing service annually requires ongoing administration.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Physical devices plus a recurring annual service per device mean ongoing renewal tracking, device inventory/lending management, and eventual hardware replacement.

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** General. Connectivity runs over the T-Mobile network; data security depends on how the organization configures and manages the connected devices and any lending program.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** The recurring $120/year-per-device service makes ongoing budgeting essential; connectivity is sustainable only as long as the annual service is renewed and the org remains within coverage.
