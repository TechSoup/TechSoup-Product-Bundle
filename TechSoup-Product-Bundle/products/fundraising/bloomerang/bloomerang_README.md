## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Bloomerang is a modern donor management CRM focused heavily on donor retention. It visualizes donor engagement over time and integrates easily with modern marketing tools.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to all registered charities.
- Discount structures are often tied to organizational budget or "startup" status.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Simple data migration.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Extremely user-friendly interface designed for fundraisers, not IT staff.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. High automation reduces manual entry.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Standard cloud security, PCI compliant payment integrations.
- **AI Functionality:** "Generative AI for Fundraisers" feature to help draft acknowledgment letters and appeals.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Excellent user adoption rates reduce training costs, though the per-record pricing scales steeply for large databases.
