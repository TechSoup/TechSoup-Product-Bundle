## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Arreva (formerly Telosa) provides Exceed! fundraising and donor management software to nonprofits through TechSoup, offered as a donation or a discount based on operating budget. It manages donors, prospects, board members, and volunteers in a single database and tracks gifts, pledges, dues, memorials, and honor gifts. Standard and custom reports with charts and graphs, plus QuickBooks and Constant Contact / VerticalResponse integration, help organizations report on and grow their fundraising.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries in the US.
- Budget tiers determine the offer: organizations with budgets of $50K or less are eligible for the donated version; organizations with budgets greater than $50K and under $500K are eligible for the discounted version.
- Quantity limits: one Exceed! Basic per fiscal year and one Exceed! Premier over the organization's lifetime.
- Exclusion: organizations that already own Arreva products are ineligible for Premier.
- Payment model: a donation (or discount) with a TechSoup administrative fee applied.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** A Windows-capable environment to run Exceed!; QuickBooks and Constant Contact / VerticalResponse accounts if those integrations are used; Microsoft Office and Outlook for export integration.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. Setting up a single donor database, configuring integrations, and building custom reports requires some staff comfort with data management.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. The organization maintains its donor database and records (Premier supports up to 2,500 records), manages integrations, and handles ongoing data hygiene.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Consolidates donor, prospect, board, and volunteer data into one database, so access controls and data governance over that central record set are the organization's responsibility. No specific certifications are claimed in this offer.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** With the donated/discounted tiers capped at budgets under $500K and Premier limited to one per lifetime, Arreva fits smaller nonprofits establishing donor management; organizations should plan for growth beyond the record and budget limits.
