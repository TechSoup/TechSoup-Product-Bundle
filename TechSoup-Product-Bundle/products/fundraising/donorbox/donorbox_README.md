## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Donorbox is a powerful, lightweight donation platform that can be embedded into any website in minutes. It supports recurring donations, localized currencies, and a wide variety of payment methods including Apple Pay and Google Pay.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- **Status:** Open to nonprofits, individuals, and organizations worldwide.
- **Verification:** Fast setup via Stripe or PayPal. Advanced features may require nonprofit validation.
- **Fees:** Charges a 1.75% platform fee (capped for large donations) plus standard processing fees. Offers "Ultra-Low" fees for larger nonprofits.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Low. Embed code or hosted donation page.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Minimal. Automated tax receipts and donor management tools.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** PCI Level 1 compliant; secure data encryption.
- **AI Functionality:** Donorbox includes AI-generated personalized thank-you messages and donor behavior insights in its Pro and Premium tiers.
