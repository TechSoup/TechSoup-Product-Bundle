## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Bonterra (formerly Social Solutions/Apricot) is a specialized case management software built specifically for human services nonprofits. It focuses on tracking participant outcomes, managing workflows, and complying with complex grant reporting requirements.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to all nonprofits, but primarily targets direct-service organizations (Human Services, Criminal Justice, Youth Development).
- No standard "free tier"; discounts are negotiated based on organizational budget and scale.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Clean historical data for migration.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. Less complex than Salesforce but still requires specialized training to configure forms and reports.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Custom report building requires ongoing administrative effort.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** SOC2 compliant. Strong focus on HIPAA compliance and PII masking, critical for human services.
- **AI Functionality:** Limited generative AI. Focuses on deterministic outcome measurement and risk scoring.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High for organizations that require strict adherence to federal grant reporting standards (e.g., HUD, HMIS), though cost scaling can be an issue for very small teams.
