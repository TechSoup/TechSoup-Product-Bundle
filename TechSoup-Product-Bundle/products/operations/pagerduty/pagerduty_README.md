## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
PagerDuty offers nonprofits 10 free licenses of its incident response platform through its Impact Pricing program. It acts as the central hub for operations and IT teams to manage on-call schedules, automate incident routing, and orchestrate critical response workflows when systems go down.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) charities.
- Requires application via the PagerDuty.org portal and verification of nonprofit status.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Integration with existing monitoring tools (e.g., New Relic, AWS CloudWatch, Datadog) to trigger alerts.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. Setting up integration webhooks is straightforward, but defining escalation policies and on-call rotations requires strong operational planning.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Once the rotations and integrations are mapped, the system handles the heavy lifting autonomously.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** SOC2 Type 2, ISO 27001. Single Sign-On (SSO) enforced.
- **AI Functionality:** Event Intelligence uses machine learning to group related alerts, reducing noise and preventing alert fatigue during major incidents.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. 10 licenses are usually more than enough for a nonprofit IT/Ops team, keeping the ongoing cost strictly at zero while drastically improving system resilience.
