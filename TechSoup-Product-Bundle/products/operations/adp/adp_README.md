## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, ADP is an online payroll service that lets U.S. organizations process payroll, direct-deposit paychecks, file taxes, and generate reports at a 25%+ discount with two months free. It makes secure electronic employee payments and automatically calculates, deposits, and files payroll taxes. ADP also provides 15+ payroll and employee data reports and integrates with tools like QuickBooks and Peachtree.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Budget Cap:** Any budget size is eligible.
- **Regions:** U.S. organizations only; U.S. territories are not eligible.
- **Quantity Limit:** One ADP product per fiscal year.
- **New vs. Existing:** Existing ADP subscribers are ineligible.
- **Tiers:** Two service tiers based on employee count (49 or fewer vs. 50 or more).
- **Payment Model:** Discounted service; pricing combines a TechSoup admin fee with ADP's ongoing fees.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Employee banking details for direct deposit and current tax/registration information; optional integration with an existing accounting ledger (QuickBooks, Peachtree).
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Payroll runs in ADP's hosted service; setup is primarily a finance/HR task rather than an IT one.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Payroll requires disciplined per-cycle data entry, and tax filings depend on keeping employee and rate data current.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Makes secure electronic employee payments through ADP's hosted platform; handles sensitive payroll and tax data on the vendor's infrastructure.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. ADP is an established payroll provider with email and phone support at no cost, but the tier is fixed by employee count and the offer renews under combined admin and ADP fees.
