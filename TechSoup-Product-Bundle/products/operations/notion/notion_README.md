## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Notion for Nonprofits provides a 50% discount on the Notion Plus plan, an integrated workspace for wikis, notes, documents, and project management. Teams can build internal wikis with permissions, run projects through Kanban boards, lists, and tables, and collaborate on notes and documents in one place. Ready-made templates help organizations standardize how they organize their work.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries (validated via TechSoup).
- **Budget Tiers:** A smaller-org plan applies to budgets up to $1M; a larger-org plan applies to budgets over $1M.
- **Quantity Limit:** Up to 3 products over the organization's lifetime.
- **Exclusions:** Hospitals and auxiliaries are not eligible.
- **Payment Model:** Discounted subscription on the Plus plan.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond web/mobile access.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. The open-ended workspace structure requires periodic content cleanup and permission review.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Uses SSL and encryption at rest, with VPC support for tighter network isolation.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Highly flexible across wiki, notes, and project-management needs, making it adaptable as the organization grows.
