## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Foxit provides PDF editing and e-signature software (PDF Editor+ and eSign Business) at significant nonprofit discounts through TechSoup, with payment made directly to Foxit. Staff can edit text and images, reorder, merge, and delete pages, and convert or export documents to Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. eSign Business adds legally binding e-signatures with envelope tracking, and an Admin Console centralizes user and license management.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Budget Cap:** Annual operating budget must be $10M or less.
- **Quantity Limit:** Up to 5 of each product per organization per fiscal year.
- **New vs. Existing:** Existing Foxit subscribers are ineligible until their current subscription expires.
- **Payment Model:** Discounted subscription accessed through TechSoup; payment is made directly to Foxit.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Windows or macOS.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Per-user licensing and the Admin Console make deployment straightforward.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. License assignment is handled centrally through the Admin Console.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Supports password protection, encryption, and audit trails, with compliance aligned to HIPAA, GDPR, and ESIGN/UETA for e-signatures.
- **AI Functionality:** Includes an integrated generative AI assistant to summarize, rewrite, and redact document content.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. A mature Acrobat alternative with centralized administration, though the TechSoup offer is subscription-based and tied to annual renewal.
