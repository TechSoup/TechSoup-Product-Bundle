## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
OnBoard is board management software for hosting secure board and committee meetings and centralizing board activities, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup at a 50% discount. It provides an agenda and board book builder with real-time collaboration, plus notes and annotations synced across devices. Web conferencing runs through Zoom integration, and unlimited storage with per-file permissions keeps governance materials organized and access-controlled.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Restricted to organizations with an annual budget of $1M or more.
- Requires a 3-year service contract.
- Minimum of 10-15 users.
- Existing OnBoard subscribers are ineligible.
- Payment model is a discount paid to OnBoard directly: 50% off Essentials/Premium/Ultimate ($200-$400/user/year), with a $0.00 TechSoup admin fee.
- Available in all regions.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Supported devices (iOS, Android, Windows, web) and a Zoom account for the web conferencing integration.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to Moderate. Hosted SaaS; setting up users, permissions, and the Zoom integration is light IT work.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. The vendor handles the platform; ongoing work is administrative (building board books, managing per-file permissions).

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Per-file permissions provide granular access control over sensitive governance materials.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Strong fit for established boards, but the required 3-year contract, 10-15 user minimum, and $1M+ budget floor make this a commitment best suited to larger, financially stable organizations.
