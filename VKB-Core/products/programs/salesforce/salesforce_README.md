## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Through the "Power of Us" program, Salesforce donates 10 Enterprise Edition licenses to eligible nonprofits and heavily discounts additional licenses. The Nonprofit Cloud provides architecture for case management, program tracking, and impact metrics.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to recognized 501(c)(3) charities globally.
- Higher education and K-12 have separate matching programs.
- Requires standard documentation upload via the Salesforce portal.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None.
- **IT Expertise Required:** High. Salesforce is notoriously complex to customize. Most orgs require hiring a Salesforce Consultant for initial rollout.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** High. Requires a dedicated Salesforce Administrator to manage updates, flows, and user roles to prevent data debt.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Highly secure. SOC2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, HIPAA compliant. Field-level security and strict role hierarchies.
- **AI Functionality:** Einstein AI included on higher tiers (predictive scoring, generative case summaries) but requires immense data hygiene to be useful.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Medium. While 10 licenses are free, implementation costs can reach tens of thousands of dollars, and ecosystem apps often incur high recurring fees.
