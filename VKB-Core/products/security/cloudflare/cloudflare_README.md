## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Project Galileo provides free enterprise-grade cybersecurity (DDoS protection, WAF, CDN) to vulnerable targets, including human rights organizations, journalism outlets, and democracy protection nonprofits.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Not open to all nonprofits. Applicants must work in human rights, journalism, or democracy-building and be subject to cyber threats due to their work.
- Reviewed by third-party partners (e.g., EFF, Access Now).
- NTEE Codes: Usually heavily skewed toward W (Public & Societal Benefit), P (Human Services), and R (Civil Rights).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Ability to change authoritative DNS nameservers.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. DNS management and understanding WAF rules.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Cloudflare's automated mitigation handles most threats without intervention.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Enterprise-grade edge protection, SOC2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS.
- **AI Functionality:** AI-based bot management and threat intelligence.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Extremely generous free tier with low risk of bait-and-switch due to the project's public interest mandate.
