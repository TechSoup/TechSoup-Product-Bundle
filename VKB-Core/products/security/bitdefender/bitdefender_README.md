## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Bitdefender donates antivirus and endpoint security to eligible nonprofits and libraries through TechSoup, spanning Windows/Mac antivirus, Internet Security, GravityZone Business Security, and Android Mobile Security across several device-count tiers. Organizations pay only a small per-product administrative fee and can receive up to 50 device licenses per fiscal year.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Budget Cap:** Annual operating budget of $1.4M or less for the donated versions.
- **Quantity Limit:** Up to 50 device licenses per fiscal year.
- **Excluded:** Schools and colleges, and political or advocacy organizations, are not eligible.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Stand up the GravityZone management console (cloud-hosted or on-premises) before deploying agents to endpoints.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate — comfortable installing endpoint agents and applying security policies.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate; periodic policy tuning and review of threat reports.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Layered next-gen endpoint protection with firewall, phishing defense, behavior-based detection, and content/device control across Windows, Mac, and Android.
- **AI Functionality:** Machine-learning models detect zero-day ransomware and advanced threats.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. The centralized GravityZone console scales well for small-to-mid NGOs and renews annually through TechSoup.
