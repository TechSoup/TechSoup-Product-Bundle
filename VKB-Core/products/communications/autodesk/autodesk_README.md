## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Through its Technology Impact Program, Autodesk donates professional design software for 3D, CAD, BIM, and animation to nonprofits using design for environmental or social good, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup. The donation spans the Architecture, Engineering & Construction Collection (BIM/CAD), AutoCAD 2D/3D drafting with specialized toolsets, and Fusion 360 cloud-enabled CAD/CAM/CAE, along with Media & Entertainment and Product Design Collections.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries in the US.
- Organizations must have annual budgets of $10M or less.
- Up to 10 subscriptions per fiscal year.
- Excludes political, advocacy, employee-benefit, and non-secular religious organizations.
- Organizations that purchased Autodesk products in the past 3 years need an exception.
- Donated software; a TechSoup administrative fee applies.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Workstations capable of running heavy 3D/CAD/animation software; Fusion 360 is cloud-enabled and Collections include 25 GB of A360 cloud storage.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. Provisioning subscriptions and managing seats is straightforward, but the applications themselves are professional design tools.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Subscriptions renew annually and require re-qualification within program limits.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Cloud-connected subscriptions with A360 cloud storage and priority technical support.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Reliable for design-focused missions, though the $10M budget cap and 3-year prior-purchase rule limit who can participate over time.
