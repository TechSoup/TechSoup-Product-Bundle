## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Mailchimp is a widely used mass email and marketing automation platform. They offer a standard 15% discount for verified nonprofits across all paid tiers.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) organizations.
- Requires submitting documentation directly to their billing team.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Domain ownership to configure DKIM, SPF, and DMARC records to ensure email deliverability.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate for initial domain authentication setup; Low for ongoing use by communications staff.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low, mostly revolving around list hygiene to prevent hitting higher pricing tiers.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Standard data encryption, GDPR compliance tools built-in.
- **AI Functionality:** Intuit Assist generative AI can draft email copy and suggest subject lines.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Low/Medium. While popular, Mailchimp's pricing model charges based on total contacts (including unsubscribed), which can cause costs to balloon quickly for growing nonprofits unless active list hygiene is maintained.
