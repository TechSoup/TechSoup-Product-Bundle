## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Genially is an online content creation tool for interactive presentations, infographics, learning materials, and reports, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup as a donated or steeply discounted Master plan. It supports interactive presentations, reports, and dossiers, animated video presentations, and interactive infographics. Content can be shared from the cloud or exported to PDF, JPEG, or HTML, and gamification features include quizzes and escape rooms.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Organizations with annual budgets of $1M or less receive a donated smaller-org Master plan; those above $1M receive an 85% discounted larger-org plan.
- Unlimited subscriptions per fiscal year.
- Existing paid Genially subscribers are eligible only after their current subscription expires.
- Payment model: donated plan carries a $30 admin fee to TechSoup; the discounted plan carries a $10 admin fee plus the 85% discounted rate paid directly to Genially.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None; cloud-based and accessed through a web browser.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. No installation; content lives in the cloud with export options to PDF, JPEG, or HTML.
