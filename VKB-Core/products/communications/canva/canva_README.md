## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Canva for Nonprofits unlocks the premium features of Canva Pro for up to 50 users at no cost. This provides access to premium templates, millions of stock photos, brand kits, and background removal tools, allowing non-designers to create professional graphics.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to registered nonprofits, social impact organizations, and public health organizations.
- Excludes schools (which use Canva for Education) and government entities.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Zero. Fully cloud-based web application.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Zero IT overhead. Brand managers will need to maintain the "Brand Kit" to ensure design consistency.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** SOC2, ISO 27001. SSO available on enterprise tiers but standard nonprofit tier relies on standard web security.
- **AI Functionality:** "Magic Studio" includes text-to-image generation, magic eraser, and auto-resize. Users must ensure generated content aligns with brand voice.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Extremely high. Free tier is generous and significantly reduces the need for expensive Adobe Creative Cloud licenses.
