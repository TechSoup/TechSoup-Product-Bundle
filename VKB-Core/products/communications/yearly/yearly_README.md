## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Yearly is a platform for nonprofits to create digital annual and impact reports to showcase their work to internal and external stakeholders, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup as a 50% discounted subscription. It provides pre-built designed modules for stories, letters, and quotes, the ability to share financials with charts, and links directly to donation forms within reports. It comes in Pro and Premium tiers, with Premium adding PDF export.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- All organization types are eligible, with an annual operating budget of $2 million or less.
- Existing Yearly subscribers are ineligible.
- Organizations must agree to a one-year service contract and be willing to provide a case study.
- Payment model: a 50% discount on a one-year subscription is paid directly to Yearly, with a TechSoup admin fee of $42.00 (Pro) or $58.00 (Premium).

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None; cloud-based report builder with custom branding for fonts, colors, photos, and logos.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Unlimited annual and interim reports under a one-year subscription.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Cloud-based reporting platform with direct links to donation forms; specific controls are not detailed in the offer data.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Well-suited to recurring annual and impact reporting, subject to the $2M budget cap and one-year service contract.
