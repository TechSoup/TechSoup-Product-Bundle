## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) provides reliable email and SMS marketing. They offer an NGO program with a 15% discount for verified organizations.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to registered 501(c)(3) nonprofits.
- Requires direct application via support with proof of status.
- Discount usually applies to the Enterprise tier; smaller teams should use the generous free tier.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** DNS setup for email deliverability.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate (list hygiene).

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Compliant with GDPR/CCPA.
- **AI Functionality:** AI Send Time Optimization and AI content drafting.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Unlimited contacts on all plans means costs don't scale purely by database size.
