## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Givebutter is a modern, all-in-one fundraising platform that includes donation forms, peer-to-peer campaigns, event ticketing, and a built-in CRM. It is famous for its "0% platform fee" model, where the service is funded by optional donor tips.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- **Status:** Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits, as well as individuals and groups (though some features like tax-receipting require verified nonprofit status).
- **Geography:** Primarily serves the United States and Canada.
- **Verification:** Simple onboarding with Stripe-powered verification. For "Givebutter Plus" features, additional nonprofit verification may be required.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Extremely low. Can be set up in minutes without coding.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Intuitive drag-and-drop interface.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Automatic receipting and donor tracking.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** PCI Level 1 compliant via Stripe integration.
- **AI Functionality:** Givebutter Plus includes AI-powered marketing tools and automated donor outreach workflows.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Rapidly growing platform with deep venture backing and a sustainable tip-based business model.
