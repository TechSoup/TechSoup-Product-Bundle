## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Little Green Light is cloud-based donor management software, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, that manages donors, volunteers, members, events, and fundraising campaigns from a single interface. It supports customizable constituent categories and gift and pledge tracking, including soft credits and honor/memory gifts, so organizations can keep a complete picture of their relationships. Complex joined and nested queries plus custom and scheduled reports help teams segment constituents and report on fundraising.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits.
- Limit of one product per organization lifetime.
- Existing and former Little Green Light subscribers are ineligible.
- The offer is delivered as subscription credits sized by budget tier ($486 for budgets of $100k or less; $125 for budgets over $100k) applied toward the subscription, which starts at $45/mo.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** A web browser; accounts for any integrated services in use (Constant Contact, Mailchimp, QuickBooks Online, PayPal).
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. Building customizable constituent categories, complex joined/nested queries, and custom or scheduled reports rewards some staff comfort with data management.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. As a hosted cloud service, the vendor handles infrastructure; ongoing effort is mostly data hygiene, gift/pledge entry, and acknowledgements.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Cloud-hosted donor database; access governance over constituent and gift records is the organization's responsibility. No specific certifications are claimed in this offer.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** With one product per organization lifetime and a low-cost monthly subscription, Little Green Light suits small-to-mid-sized nonprofits standardizing on a single donor management system, but organizations should weigh the ongoing subscription cost after the initial credit.
