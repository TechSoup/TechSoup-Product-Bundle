## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Zeffy (formerly Simplyk) is the only 100% free fundraising platform for nonprofits. They charge zero platform fees and zero transaction fees, meaning nonprofits keep 100% of every donation. The platform is funded entirely by voluntary tips from donors at checkout.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- **Status:** Strictly limited to registered nonprofits and charities.
- **Geography:** Available to nonprofits in the United States and Canada.
- **Verification:** Requires proof of nonprofit status (e.g., 501(c)(3) determination letter).

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Low. Web-based platform with embeddable widgets.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Minimal. Handles all processing, receipting, and data management for free.
