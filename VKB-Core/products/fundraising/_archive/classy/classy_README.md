## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Classy (a GoFundMe company) is an enterprise-grade fundraising suite designed for high-growth and large-scale nonprofits. It offers sophisticated tools for peer-to-peer fundraising, event management (Classy Live), and deep CRM integrations.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- **Status:** Targeted at registered 501(c)(3) nonprofits.
- **Verification:** Managed via internal compliance team; requires proof of legal status.
- **Budget Restriction:** Typically best suited for organizations with annual revenues over $1M due to the base subscription cost.
