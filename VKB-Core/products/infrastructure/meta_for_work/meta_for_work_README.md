## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

Meta for Work provides the Meta Quest Pro virtual and mixed reality headset as a hardware donation to give nonprofit staff, volunteers, and constituents immersive experiences. Offered to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries through TechSoup, it delivers standalone VR and mixed reality with passthrough, supports immersive learning and training, and runs on a Snapdragon XR2+ processor with 256 GB of storage. It is well suited to organizations exploring VR-based training or constituent engagement.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- **Eligibility:** Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Region exclusions:** U.S. territories are not eligible.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Physical headset that must be unboxed, charged, set up, and paired with the appropriate apps/accounts before use. Plan for content or training applications to actually use it.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. Initial device setup is consumer-grade, but deploying VR for staff/constituents, managing accounts, and supporting users requires hands-on attention.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate-to-High. As a legacy device, it carries a one-year limited warranty and will receive no support or updates after certain dates, so long-term reliability and software compatibility are concerns.
