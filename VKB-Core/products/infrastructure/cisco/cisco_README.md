## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup via two tracks. The Cisco Meraki **Discount** track provides 50%-off cloud-managed networking equipment, security cameras, sensors, and licenses to 501(c)(3) orgs with budgets up to $50M, covering MX security appliances (firewall, VPN), MS-series PoE switches, MR/CW-series Wi-Fi access points, MV-series cameras, and MT-series environmental sensors. The **Donation** track instead donates Cisco Meraki networking equipment, security cameras, and sensors (with 5-year licenses included) to mission-qualifying 501(c)(3) orgs with budgets up to $20M, spanning donated MX appliances, MS/Catalyst switches, CW-series Wi-Fi 6E/7 access points, MG cellular gateways, and Z-series teleworker gateways.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to US 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries, validated through TechSoup.
- **Discount track:** annual budget of $50M or less.
- **Donation track:** mission-qualifying orgs with budgets up to $20M, with up to $8,000 in admin fees per fiscal year; includes Systems Manager device licenses and an Employee Product Donation Program.
- Schools and government agencies are ineligible for the discount track.
- US territories (except Puerto Rico) are ineligible.
- Orgs eligible for donations should use the donation track; orgs that do not qualify for donations can use the 50%-off discount track instead.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Physical premises requiring networking, with appropriate power and cabling for the hardware being deployed.
- **IT Expertise Required:** High. Configuring and operating Meraki dashboards and switching/security appliances typically requires dedicated IT staff or a managed service provider.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** High. Requires firmware patching and attention to license terms; cloud-managed Meraki gear depends on active licenses to keep functioning. Donated gear includes 5-year licenses.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Strong network security. Hardware includes MX security appliances providing firewall and VPN capabilities, plus advanced security cloud licenses.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer. The offer is centered on networking hardware and licensing.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Medium/High. The hardware is durable, but recurring license renewals for cloud-managed Meraki gear can become an unbudgeted cost once included terms expire.
