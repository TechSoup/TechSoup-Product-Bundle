## HP Inc. Expert Knowledge
- This offer is U.S.-only and excludes U.S. territories, so confirm the shipping organization is in a U.S. state before recommending it. Orders shipping to California also incur a per-laptop California eWaste fee that should be budgeted on top of the device admin fee.
- There are two distinct tracks: per-unit discounted hardware (admin fees $183-$1,699) and free ($0 admin) access to the HP.com Nonprofit Store for ongoing discounts up to 68%. Make sure the client requests Nonprofit Store access to maximize savings on repeat purchases rather than only buying one-off discounted units.
