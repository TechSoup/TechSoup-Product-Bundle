## Level 1 (Quick Glance)

HP Inc. offers nonprofits discounted laptops, desktops, all-in-ones, monitors, printers, and docking stations through TechSoup, plus access to the HP.com Nonprofit Store for additional discounted hardware and services. Through this offer, 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries can buy discounted desktops, laptops, and workstations (up to 68% off), discounted monitors, accessories, and care packs, and USB-C and Thunderbolt docking stations. Most hardware ships with multi-year warranties, making it a fit for organizations refreshing aging endpoint fleets.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)

- **Eligibility:** Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries; U.S. organizations only.
- **Region exclusions:** U.S. territories are not eligible.
- **Budget cap:** Any budget size (no operating-budget ceiling).
- **Quantity limits:** Unlimited devices may be purchased.
- **Payment model:** Discount offer. Per-unit TechSoup admin fees apply ($183-$1,699 depending on the device); separately, the HP.com Nonprofit Store can be accessed for a $0 admin fee with up to 68% off.
- **Additional fees:** A California eWaste fee applies per laptop for orders shipping to California.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)

- **Technical Prerequisites:** Standard hardware procurement and on-site setup. Devices arrive as physical endpoints that must be unboxed, imaged/configured, and deployed to staff; printers and docking stations require physical connection and driver installation.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Moderate. No specialized skills are needed to purchase, but provisioning, imaging, and connecting docks/printers to a network requires basic IT capacity.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. As physical devices, units need ongoing care: warranty/care-pack tracking, eventual repair or replacement, printer consumables (ink), and end-of-life disposal (note the California eWaste fee).

### Embedded Parameters

- **Security Posture:** General. The offer is hardware procurement; security depends on how the organization configures and patches the devices it receives.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Hardware with multi-year warranties and ongoing discounted purchasing via the HP.com Nonprofit Store supports a sustainable, repeatable device-refresh cycle, though units will still age out and require eventual replacement.
