## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Through TechSoup, Asana provides nonprofits a 50% discount on the Asana Starter or Advanced work management subscription to help teams coordinate work and meet deadlines. Teams can organize work across multiple project views (lists, boards, calendars, and timelines), use custom fields and project templates to standardize processes, and rely on task dependencies to map out complex, multi-step workflows. The platform supports both web and mobile collaboration so staff can stay coordinated from anywhere.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries (validated through TechSoup).
- **Budget Cap:** Any budget size is eligible.
- **Quantity Limit:** Request one product covering all team members; the subscription supports 2 to 1,000 users.
- **New vs. Existing:** Existing Asana subscribers may renew under the discount.
- **Exclusions:** Certain religious, hospital/health, mutual, fraternal, advocacy, and government organizations are not eligible.
- **Payment Model:** Discounted subscription (50% off) rather than a donation.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond web/mobile access for staff.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. The intuitive UI requires very little technical training to adopt project views and templates.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Keeping workspaces, custom fields, and saved views clean is more a managerial/cultural task than an IT one.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Offers data access controls and comment-only project sharing to govern who can edit versus view work.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** High. Low maintenance burden and strong staff adoption make it a durable backbone for operational coordination.
