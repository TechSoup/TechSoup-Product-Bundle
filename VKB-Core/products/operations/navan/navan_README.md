## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Navan is a travel and expense management platform, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup as a promotional incentive: $500 in Navan Rewards Points for organizations that create a Navan Business account and complete a qualifying full-price hotel booking within 90 days. The platform handles flight and hotel travel booking alongside automated expense categorization and reporting. The Business plan is free to sign up for, and the rewards points apply toward future bookings.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Any budget size is eligible.
- Limit of one offer per organization lifetime.
- Existing Navan customers are ineligible.
- Sign-up must use a custom-domain email (not a free/personal address).
- Rewards require a $100+ full-price hotel booking completed within 90 days; the $500 in points is an incentive, not a software donation or discount.
- $0 TechSoup admin fee.
- US organizations only.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** A custom-domain organizational email address to register the Navan Business account.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. Cloud/mobile platform with no installation; sign-up is self-service.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Hosted SaaS; ongoing use is a finance/operations task, not an IT one.
