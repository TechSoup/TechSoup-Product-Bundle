## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
MonkeyPod is an all-in-one nonprofit platform that integrates accounting, donor management/CRM, online fundraising, email marketing, and grant management, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup at a 50% first-year discount on the Essentials plan. It combines integrated nonprofit fund accounting with a donor/constituent CRM so finance and development teams share one system. Online fundraising and donation processing plus grant lifecycle management round out the platform, with unlimited users and data.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Any budget size is eligible.
- Limit of one product per organization lifetime.
- Existing MonkeyPod customers are ineligible.
- The 50% discount applies to the first year only.
- Payment model is a discount paid to MonkeyPod directly, with a $10 TechSoup admin fee.
- US organizations only.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond a web browser; it is a hosted platform.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to Moderate. No installation, but migrating accounting and donor data and configuring fund accounting benefits from finance/operations staff comfort.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Hosted SaaS; the vendor handles updates, while your team maintains data quality across the integrated modules.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Not detailed in this offer; as a financial/donor platform, evaluate the vendor's controls directly.

- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Consolidating accounting, CRM, fundraising, and grants in one tool reduces silos, but budget for the post-first-year price step-up once the 50% discount lapses.
