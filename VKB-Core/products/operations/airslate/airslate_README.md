## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, airSlate provides discounted document management, e-signature, and workflow automation software via pdfFiller and signNow (Business Premium and Enterprise plans). pdfFiller handles PDF editing, a library of fillable forms, and document management, while signNow delivers legally binding e-signatures across platforms. Court-admissible audit trails and document history support recordkeeping for signed documents.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries (U.S.).
- **Budget Cap:** Any budget size is eligible.
- **Quantity Limit:** One of each airSlate product per fiscal year.
- **New vs. Existing:** Existing pdfFiller, signNow, or airSlate subscribers are ineligible for their respective offers.
- **Payment Model:** Discounted subscription; an annual admin fee is required to maintain the discount.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Web and mobile access; documents and reusable templates set up within pdfFiller and signNow.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to Moderate. Basic e-signature and form use is straightforward, but building reusable templates, conditional fields, and payment requests (Enterprise) takes process design.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low to Moderate. Templates and branding need periodic review, and the discount must be renewed each year via the admin fee.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Court-admissible audit trails and document history provide a defensible record of who signed what and when.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Moderate. Two distinct products (pdfFiller and signNow) cover documents and signatures, but the discount lapses if the annual admin fee is not paid.
