## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Offered to nonprofits through TechSoup, BambooHR provides discounted access to its cloud-based HR management platform (Core and Pro plans) to manage employee, payroll, time, and benefit information. It centralizes HR data management and reporting and includes hiring and onboarding tools with e-signatures. The platform also covers payroll, time tracking, and benefits administration in one place.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- **Budget Cap:** Any budget size is eligible.
- **Quantity Limit:** One per organization lifetime.
- **New vs. Existing:** Existing BambooHR subscribers are ineligible.
- **Plans:** Core and Pro; the Pro plan adds performance reviews.
- **Payment Model:** Discounted subscription (20-50% off monthly fees plus 50% off setup); a credit card is required and payment is made directly to BambooHR, with a TechSoup admin fee of $75-$250.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Employee records to import; web and mobile access for staff and managers.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. BambooHR is hosted, so deployment is primarily an HR-process task rather than an IT one.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Employee data, onboarding workflows, and benefits/time records require ongoing HR upkeep.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Hosted HR platform handling sensitive employee, payroll, and benefit data; includes e-signatures for hiring and onboarding documents.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Moderate. A capable HR system of record, but the offer is one-per-lifetime and excludes existing subscribers, so the discount cannot be repeated once used.
