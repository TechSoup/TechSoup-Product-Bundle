## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Intuit donates QuickBooks Desktop accounting software through TechSoup, including the Premier, Mac, and Accountant editions and the Nonprofit Edition, for a small administrative fee. The Nonprofit Edition ships with an IRS Form 990-aligned chart of accounts and supports reporting by donor or grant alongside budget-vs-actuals planning. Premier lets organizations choose among six industry editions at install, while the Accountant edition can switch among all QuickBooks editions. The desktop products support multi-user network access for up to 5 simultaneous users.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Valid 501(c)(3) organizations (U.S. only); the program also serves special programs (SBDC/SCORE/WBC).
- **Budget Cap:** Operating budget under $10 million.
- **Quantity Limit:** One product per fiscal year; one subscription product per organization lifetime.
- **New vs. Existing:** Desktop products are available only if the organization previously received a QuickBooks desktop product through TechSoup.
- **Exclusions:** Certain health, political, and advocacy org types are ineligible.
- **Payment Model:** Donation through TechSoup for an administrative fee.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** Windows PC (for Premier/Accountant) or Mac, running a supported operating system; a local network for multi-user company-file sharing.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low to install, but accounting expertise is needed to configure the chart of accounts and donor/grant tracking properly.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Moderate. Requires manual backups, and IT must manage company-file sharing when up to 5 users access it over a local network.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Data is stored locally, so security relies entirely on the nonprofit's internal endpoint protection and physical access controls.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Low/Medium. Intuit is steadily shifting toward QuickBooks Online subscriptions, and the desktop offer is restricted to prior desktop recipients, so the long-term path narrows as support windows close.
