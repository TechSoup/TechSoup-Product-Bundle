## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
QuickBooks Online is the industry standard for small-to-medium nonprofit accounting, helping organizations manage invoices, reports, and the tracking of expenses, contributions, and payments. Through TechSoup, Intuit offers subscriptions in the Plus, Essentials, and Advanced editions, featuring an IRS Form 990-aligned chart of accounts and donor/grant contribution and spending reports. Budget planning tools and 65+ built-in business reports round out the financial picture, with mobile access on iOS and Android.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Open to 501(c)(3) charities (U.S. only), verified exclusively via TechSoup.
- **Budget Cap:** Operating budgets below $10 million for certain offers.
- **Editions:** Available in Plus, Essentials, and Advanced tiers.
- **Special-Program Limit:** The Essentials free tier is limited to SBDC, SCORE, and WBC organizations for classroom training.
- **Eligibility Notes:** Some religious organizations are eligible.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** A basic chart of accounts; existing financial data can be imported from Excel, CSV, or QuickBooks Desktop.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low IT requirement, but a high accounting/CPA requirement to properly configure class tracking for nonprofit fund accounting.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Medium. Requires consistent financial reconciliation and account mapping.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** 256-bit SSL encryption, enforced MFA, and automated backups.
- **AI Functionality:** Minor automated categorization of expenses based on historical data.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Very high. As the default nonprofit accounting platform, staff and auditors who understand the system are easy to find, and data import paths ease migration.
