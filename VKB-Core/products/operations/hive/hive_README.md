## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Hive is project management and collaboration software for coordinating projects, tasks, and team work across desktop, web, and mobile, offered to nonprofits through TechSoup at 15-50% discounts depending on plan. It provides flexible project views with multi-project overviews, time management and workload visualization, and built-in team email/messaging with Hive Notes for collaboration. Unlimited file storage and integrations with Google Drive, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Salesforce, and Okta let it slot into an existing stack.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Any budget size is eligible.
- Limit of one discounted product per fiscal year.
- Requires a one-year service contract with Hive.
- Payment model is a discount paid directly to Hive (Starter 50% off, Teams 30% off, Enterprise 15% off) with a $0 TechSoup admin fee.
- Available in all regions.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond supported desktop, web, or mobile devices.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. As a hosted SaaS tool, setup is largely configuration and inviting users.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low to Moderate. Day-to-day upkeep is managerial (keeping projects organized); configuring integrations to Salesforce, Okta, or Teams may need some IT involvement.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Supports Okta integration for identity/SSO; other controls are not detailed in this offer.
- **AI Functionality:** Hive includes AI automation of repeated processes and risk identification within projects.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** Broad integrations reduce data silos, but note the required one-year service contract and the steep discount tiering by plan when budgeting for renewal.
