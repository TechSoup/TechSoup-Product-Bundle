## Claris FileMaker 19 Platform Expert Knowledge
- The license is fixed at 5 users with 3 FileMaker Server licenses, and only one license (annual or perpetual) may be requested per fiscal year (July 1 - June 30). Plan seat counts up front, because you cannot stack a second license mid-year if you outgrow 5 users.
- Weigh the $540 subscription vs. $1,620 perpetual admin fee against the reality that custom apps you build are yours to maintain. For a budget-conscious client, the perpetual option avoids recurring fees but still carries the long-term support burden of any custom solution.
