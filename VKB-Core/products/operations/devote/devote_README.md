## Level 1 (Quick Glance)
Devote is a financial technology platform built for nonprofits, offered through TechSoup as a fee-free high-yield savings account plus the Catalyst plan for expense management and reporting. The savings account earns up to 2.25% APY with expanded FDIC coverage and next-day liquidity. The Catalyst plan adds restricted/unrestricted fund tracking for grant compliance, unlimited users and cards, and audit-ready reporting.

## Level 2 (Detailed Eligibility Matrix)
- Available to 501(c)(3) nonprofits and public libraries.
- Any budget size is eligible.
- Limit of one of each product (savings account and Catalyst plan) per organization lifetime.
- Existing Devote customers are eligible.
- The high-yield savings account requires a $1,000 minimum balance.
- Payment model: the savings account is free ($0); the Catalyst plan is a 40% discount with a $10 TechSoup admin fee.
- US organizations only.

## Level 3 (Implementation & Maintenance Requirements)
- **Technical Prerequisites:** None beyond opening an account; the $1,000 minimum balance is a financial, not technical, prerequisite for the savings product.
- **IT Expertise Required:** Low. As a hosted fintech platform, there is no software to install or servers to run.
- **Maintenance Overhead:** Low. Account and fund administration is a finance-team task rather than an IT one.

### Embedded Parameters
- **Security Posture:** Savings funds carry expanded FDIC coverage; specific platform security controls beyond that are not detailed in this offer.
- **AI Functionality:** None core to this offer.
- **Long-Term Sustainability:** A purpose-built nonprofit finance platform with fund tracking and audit-ready reporting supports ongoing compliance, though it concentrates banking and reporting with a single vendor.
